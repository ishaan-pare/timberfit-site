Timberfit – Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · Applies to the Timberfit Android app (package com.timberfit.blockpuzzle)

Timberfit is a wooden block puzzle game. It works fully offline, has no account or sign-in, and asks for no personal information. This page explains the small amount of technical data the app collects so that we can keep it stable and improve it.

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress (current board, best score, settings such as sound and music) is saved only on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What the app collects

Timberfit uses two Google Firebase services. They run in the background and do not affect play. Neither one receives your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

ServiceDataWhy
Firebase Analytics A random app-instance ID, device model and Android version, app version, language, country or region (derived from your IP address), and gameplay events such as a game started, lines cleared or a screen opened. To understand how the game is played and which parts need improvement.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports and error logs: the state of the app when an error happened, device model, Android version, free memory and a random installation ID. To find and fix crashes and bugs.

This data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) to Google, which processes it on our behalf. See Firebase privacy and security and the Google Privacy Policy. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash data for 90 days, after which it is deleted automatically.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or password.
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • No selling of data, and no sharing with anyone other than Google as the service provider named above.
  • No advertising in the current version. If a future version shows ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

Timberfit is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the app stops all collection and deletes the data stored on your device.
  • Reinstalling the app creates new random identifiers; old ones are not linked to you.
  • To ask about or request deletion of the technical data described above, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

When the game gains new features (for example optional accounts, leaderboards or ads) this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. The current version is always at this address.
